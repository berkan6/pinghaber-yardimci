Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Ping Haber Helper is used only by its owner for the owner's own TikTok accounts.

Data we handle

  • TikTok authorization tokens (access/refresh) and the account's public profile identifier, obtained through TikTok Login Kit with the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload.
  • Video files that the owner created, sent to the owner's TikTok inbox.

How data is stored and used

  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used solely to upload the owner's videos to the owner's drafts.
  • No personal data of any other person is collected, sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. No analytics or advertising is used.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps, and can delete the stored tokens from their computer.

Contact

Contact the owner through TikTok @pinghaber.
